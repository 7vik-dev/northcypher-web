03 — FAQ4 questions people ask

Questions
people ask.

01

Are these tools really free?

Yes. No account, no card, no quota on the web tools. They’re a free, public slice of the same threat intelligence our analysts use on paid engagements, a way to see what NorthCypher does before you talk to us.

02

Do you store what I check?

No. Lookups are counted in aggregate so we know which tools get used, but we don’t keep a record of what you looked up, and checking a value never reports it anywhere.

03

How do the checks work?

The TLS, header, ASN, typosquat and email checks run live on our servers with no third-party key. The IP and domain fraud scores draw on continuously-updated threat data. Treat a score as a strong signal to step up verification, not a single yes/no gate.

04

Can I get this at scale or in my product?

The web tools are for interactive checks. If you need volume lookups, an API, or this wired into your own systems, that’s what the full NorthCypher platform is for, request a demo and we’ll scope it.
