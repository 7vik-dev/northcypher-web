04 — Fig. 03 · what you're paying forFour questions
01

What do I pay for?

Access for the period you pick, 30 days or a year. Nothing renews by itself; buy another period whenever you want to keep going.

02

Which emails can register?

Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo or your company mailbox. Temporary and disposable addresses are refused.

03

Do I see the actual passwords?

On Free, passwords, IDs and other secrets are masked. Starter and Pro show the record as it was found, so every paid account is protected by an authenticator, and every lookup is logged against the account that ran it.
